— AWS Well-Architected, TOGAF ADM, the C4 Model, ArchiMate, Domain-Driven
Design and the 12-Factor App — with concise summaries and practical pointers.

## What it includes

- **AWS Well-Architected Framework** — six pillars and review questions.
- **TOGAF ADM** — phases A through H with deliverables.
- **C4 Model** — Context, Container, Component and Code.
- **ArchiMate** — Business, Application and Technology layers.
- **Domain-Driven Design** — bounded contexts, ubiquitous language, patterns.
- **12-Factor App** — checklist with pragmatic interpretation.
- **Cynefin** — decision framework for complex environments.

## Why this matters

Frameworks are tools, not religion. A practicing architect needs a sharp,
fast-loading reference to compare them, pick the right one for a context, and
explain trade-offs to stakeholders without diving back into 200-page PDFs.
